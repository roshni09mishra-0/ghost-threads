Architecture

The path from raw chat export to resting spirit

Step 01

Local archive intake

Drop plain text or JSON exports straight from Signal, Telegram, or WhatsApp into a local folder. No tokenization on remote servers, no telemetry, and no sign-up forms.

Step 02

Gemma semantic filter

Gemma runs through Ollama on your machine. It extracts temporal commitments like "tomorrow morning", "kal sham tak", or "by end of day" across messy conversational contexts.

Step 03

TabPFN ghost scorer

TabPFN processes the extracted signals alongside conversation timestamp gaps. It performs in-context classification instantly on small tabular slices without requiring model fine-tuning.

Step 04

The ofrenda ledger

Unkept commitments are sorted by days elapsed. You get a clear list of what you abandoned so you can either finish the task or send an honest apology and let the ghost rest.

Philosophy

Why this refuses to be a cloud service

01 / Privacy

Chat exports are too intimate for someone else's server

These files hold five years of doctor appointments, late night arguments, and passwords sent in panic. You do not upload that to a cloud company to save two minutes of local setup.

02 / Connectivity

Works with the wifi completely off

Turn off your radio, sit on a delayed train, and run the entire scan. Ollama and TabPFN run in memory on your laptop without sending a single packet into the air.

03 / Ownership

You can swap the model whenever you want

No locked endpoints or changing terms. When a lighter model weight drops next month, change one line in the local script and keep your workflow intact.

Known failure mode

Where the parser still stumbles

On dry, sarcastic banter, Gemma regularly flagged jokes like "sure, I will buy you an island on Friday" as high-priority commitments. Sarcasm in multi-lingual chat groups remains an unsolved bug, so an explicit ignore list had to be added.
